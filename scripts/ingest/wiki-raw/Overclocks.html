“ Overclocks – My favourite type of clock!
— Dwarf

Overclocks are specialized weapon upgrades that can be activated by leveling weapons up during a dive. Balanced overclocks can be selected for a weapon at Weapon Levels 6 and 12, and a powerful "Unstable" overclock can be selected at Weapon Level 18. Weapons can be leveled up by focusing on weapon-specific upgrades during a dive.
