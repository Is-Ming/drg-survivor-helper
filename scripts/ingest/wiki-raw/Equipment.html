Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor Weapons
“ You've proven that one dwarf can do the work of four, if we just give you enough guns.
— Mission Control

Deep Rock Galactic provides their mining operatives with a wide variety of weapons and artifacts to help them undertake dangerous solo missions on Hoxxes IV.

Weapons are a dwarf's main line of defense and can be upgraded during a run with Upgrade Cards and Overclocks, or they can be upgraded semi-permanently between missions with Meta Upgrades. Numerous weapons and damage types become available as the player advances their classes.

Artifacts are items awarded to the dwarf throughout the run via an orbital supply drop at each stage of the dive. If activated, the supply will offer a choice of three artifact to choose from, each of those providing a chance to collect more resources, or a small conditional gameplay change (more damage while standing still, for example). Artifacts can be obtained several times during the same run, stacking their effects.

Gear are meta items kept in the player's inventory that can customize each Class Mod. Once earned, Gear will always be accessible to the player unless they're sold for Credits.

Finally, Bosco is the all-purpose drone that will accompany dwarves on all missions.

Bosco

Main article: Survivor:Bosco

APD-B317, or Bosco, is a hovering drone that accompanies the player on all dives. He acts autonomously, targeting bugs and firing volleys of bullets on his own. His weapon can be modified by equipping a Chip.

Weapons

The weapon the player starts with depends on the class mod they chose, and more can be chosen at levels 5, 15 and 25 during the dive. The available weapons in that choice will depend on what has been unlocked through completing milestones.

The weapons of Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor are categorized through two parameters: a default class, and tags that describe them and indicates what damage type they deal. The choice of a specific class mod will determine the starting weapon during a dive, and the other weapons will be drawn from a pool made of the class's unlocked default weapons (listed below).

Weapon tags are sorted in four categories:

  • Damage type – [KINETIC] [FIRE] [ELECTRIC] [COLD] [ACID] [PLASMA]
  • Family – [LIGHT] [MEDIUM] [HEAVY] [THROWABLE] [CONSTRUCT]
  • Weapon type – [PROJECTILE] [EXPLOSIVE] [DRONE] [TURRET]
  • Firing type – [PRECISE] [SPRAY] [AREA] [BEAM] [LASTING]

Weapons can have any number of tags in a category.

Artifacts

Main article: Survivor:Artifacts

Artifacts are run-specific upgrades that are obtained by completing the supply drop event. During each stage of a dive, one supply drop will spawn on the map. All terrain within its radius must be dug out as indicated by a pickaxe overlay on the terrain (note: crystals and resources will not be marked with the overlay, but must still be mined).
