Weapon Tags

Every weapon has a number of descriptive tags associated with it. Here these tags are categorized into damage type, weapon family, weapon type and firing type. Weapons can have any number of tags in a category. Some weapons can have their tags changed mid-dive by applying special Overclocks to them.

Damage type
  • [KINETIC] – basic non-elemental damage.
  • [FIRE] – inflicts Burn.
  • [ELECTRICAL] – inflicts Shock.
  • [COLD] – inflicts Slow.
  • [ACID] – inflicts Corrode.
  • [PLASMA] – projectiles bounce off walls and bugs.
Weapon family
  • [LIGHT]
  • [MEDIUM]
  • [HEAVY]
  • [THROWABLE]
  • [CONSTRUCT]
Weapon type
  • [PROJECTILE] – typically a weapon that fires bullets.
  • [EXPLOSIVE] – weapons that shoot explosives, and grenades.
  • [DRONE] – constructs that fly around and attack hostiles.
  • [TURRET] – constructs that are (typically) immobile.
  • [GROUNDZONE] - weapons create a zone on the ground that damages enemies and applies elemental stacks.
Firing type
  • [PRECISE] – damage is cast along a predictable line.
  • [SPRAY] – damage is cast into a cone.
  • [AREA] – damage is dealt in an area.
  • [BEAM] – damage is cast along a line or cone.
  • [LASTING] – weapon has some effect that remains in the world for a duration, like a construct or a damage puddle.
Special tags
These tags are obtained by upgrading weapons with certain overclocks, and enable Tag Mastery Upgrades when at least two weapons share the same tag:
  • [AKIMBO] - Gained by the Akimbo overclocks.
  • [SIDEARM] - Gained by the Sidearm overclocks.
  • [THE FAVOURITE] - Gained by The Favourite overclocks.
  • [THICK BOY] - Gained by the Thick Boy overclocks.

Explanation of Stats

  • Clip Size: How many projectiles a weapon can fire before reloading.
  • Damage: Damage inflicted per hit from a weapon.
  • Fire Rate: Projectiles fired per second, and how fast a clip is emptied.
  • Piercing: How many enemies a projectile can pierce. Larger enemies, Elites, and Bosses require higher piercing values.
  • Potency: Amount of status effect stacks applied by [FIRE] [ELECTRICAL] [COLD] and [ACID] weapons.
  • Reload Time: Downtime after a clip is emptied before it can be fired again; affects speed of deploying [CONSTRUCT] weapons.
  • Drones and Charges: The amount of drones/turrets/etc. the player can keep in their pocket at a time. When the player stands still, charges are deployed one after another; The last charge is auto-deployed if charges would over-fill.
  • Lifetime: How long [LASTING] weapons remain active. For [BEAM] weapons, this stat determines how long it takes to empty the clip - replacing the 'Fire Rate' stat.
  • Beams: Number of beams for [BEAM] weapons.
  • Explosion Radius: Maximum radius covered by [EXPLOSIVE] weapons.
  • Weapon Range: Distance covered by [BEAM] weapons, and targeting range for [PROJECTILE] weapons.
    (Note that projectile range is determined by hidden stats for projectile lifetime and speed, which regular Weapon Range upgrades do not affect. However, certain overclocks that affect Weapon Range such as High Velocity Bullets do modify these hidden stats, including the targeting range.)

The player is given a choice of upgrades to their stats every time they level up during a dive, as well as from the store where nitra can be spent on gun-specific improvements. When a stat is modified from its base value, it follows the formula

Stat = ((Base * Skill ) + Flat ) * Meta  * Overclock  * Artifact 

...where Base is a weapon's base stat, Skill  is the sum of all modifiers from Mid-dive Upgrades, Flat  is the sum of all flat (non-percentile) modifiers (like charge or beam count upgrades), Meta  is the product of Meta Upgrades * Class modifiers * Weapon Mastery modifiers, and Overclock  and Artifact  are the products of all Overclock and Artifacts modifiers. These calculations can be viewed for equipped weapons from the pause screen during a dive.

In addition to their defaults, certain sub-classes have access to all weapons of a given tag:

  • Recon (Scout) has access to his default set, plus all [LIGHT] weapons.
  • Weapons Specialist (Gunner) has access to his default set, plus all [PROJECTILE] weapons.
  • Heavy Gunner (Gunner) has access to his default set, plus all [HEAVY] weapons.
  • Interrogator (Driller) has access to his default set, plus all [FIRE] and [ACID] weapons.
  • Strong Armed (Driller) has access to his default set, plus all [THROWABLE] weapons.
  • Maintenance Worker (Engineer) has access to his default set, plus all [CONSTRUCT] weapons.
  • Demolitionist (Engineer) has access to his default set, plus all [EXPLOSIVE] weapons.
  • Gridrunner (Demolisher) has access to his default set, plus all [ELECTRICAL] weapons.
  • Operator (Demolisher) has access to his default set, plus all [DRONE] weapons.

Some Gear allow additional access to weapons given their tags:

  • DW40K adds all [BEAM] weapons to the drop pool.
  • Strange Mixture adds all [DRONE] weapons to the drop pool.
  • Support Chip adds two random damage types to the drop pool.
  • Sniper Scope adds all [PROJECTILE] weapons to the drop pool.
