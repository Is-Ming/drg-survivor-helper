The Dragonstorm Incinerator is one of the weapons available to Demolisher in DRG Survivor. It is the default weapon of the Contractor class mod.

It can also be equipped while using Driller's Interrogator class mod.

Weapon Mechanics and Interactions

The Dragonstorm Incinerator shoots a backward facing beam, leaving behind a [GROUNDZONE]. The beam and groundzone both deal [FIRE] damage and apply Burn stacks.

  • The Afterburner balanced overclock can be taken twice.

Mastery Bonuses

Completing the Dragonstorm Incinerator Weapon Mastery in Crystalline Caverns on Hazard 3+ awards the following bonus:

+12% Damage
